# Bab Al-Hara (باب الحارة): Character Reference

Reference file for the "Which Bab Al-Hara character are you?" quiz.
The model must use ONLY this file to describe characters. Do not rely on memory of the show.

## Rules for the model

The app has already scored the quiz and chosen the character. Do NOT change it.

You receive: the character `id` and the user's quiz answers.

1. Write a short narrative about the user as that character, in Levantine Arabic (شامي), dramatic like a musalsal narrator. 3–4 sentences max.
2. Reference at least two of the user's actual answers so the story feels personal.
3. Use only the traits in this file. Never invent plot events, quotes, or traits.
4. Keep it playful. Never insult the user. The "villain" characters are a funny result, not a mean one.
5. Return JSON only:

```json
{ "narrative": "..." }
```

## Characters

### abu_issam — أبو عصام (Abu Issam)
- **Role:** The neighborhood's barber and healer, its wise man (حكيم الحارة).
- **Core traits:** Patient, forgiving, calm, a peacemaker. Solves problems with words, not fists.
- **Under pressure:** Stays calm, listens to both sides, looks for the solution everyone can live with.
- **Weakness:** Forgives too much. Proud when his authority at home is challenged.
- **You're Abu Issam if:** Friends come to you for advice, and you'd rather fix a fight than win it.

### abu_shehab — العكيد أبو شهاب (Al-Eqeed Abu Shehab)
- **Role:** The strongman and protector of the neighborhood (العكيد).
- **Core traits:** Brave, decisive, honorable, few words, a lot of action. Respected by the young men.
- **Under pressure:** Takes charge immediately and protects people first.
- **Weakness:** Stubborn. Once he's angry, he doesn't back down easily.
- **You're Abu Shehab if:** You're the one people look to in a crisis, and you keep your word no matter what.

### motaz — معتز (Mo'taz)
- **Role:** Abu Issam's younger son.
- **Core traits:** Loyal, energetic, protective of family, acts fast.
- **Under pressure:** Reacts first, thinks later. Will defend his family's name on the spot.
- **Weakness:** Hot-headed. Gets into fights he could have avoided.
- **You're Mo'taz if:** You'd ride or die for your people, and patience isn't your strongest skill.

### issam — عصام (Issam)
- **Role:** Abu Issam's eldest son.
- **Core traits:** Kind-hearted, gentle, wants everyone happy.
- **Under pressure:** Gets caught in the middle and tries to please both sides.
- **Weakness:** Avoids confrontation, struggles to say no.
- **You're Issam if:** You hate conflict and somehow always end up in the middle of other people's drama.

### abu_ghaleb — أبو غالب (Abu Ghaleb)
- **Role:** The neighborhood's troublemaker.
- **Core traits:** Sneaky, petty, holds grudges, always has a scheme.
- **Under pressure:** Plots quietly instead of facing things head-on.
- **Weakness:** His schemes usually backfire on him.
- **You're Abu Ghaleb if:** You never forget who wronged you and always have a plan B (and C).

### um_zaki — أم زكي (Um Zaki)
- **Role:** The neighborhood's matchmaker (الخاطبة).
- **Core traits:** Social, talkative, connected, knows everyone's business.
- **Under pressure:** Talks her way through it and somehow knows something useful.
- **Weakness:** News travels through her faster than it should.
- **You're Um Zaki if:** You know everyone, you're the group chat's source of news, and you love setting people up.

### abu_badr — أبو بدر (Abu Badr)
- **Role:** The neighborhood's comic figure.
- **Core traits:** Funny, timid, easygoing, a follower rather than a leader.
- **Under pressure:** Panics a little, then goes along with whoever is in charge. His wife has the final word at home.
- **Weakness:** Avoids responsibility and backs down quickly.
- **You're Abu Badr if:** You're the funniest one in the group and would rather not be in charge of anything.

### abu_alnar — العكيد أبو النار (Al-Eqeed Abu Al-Nar)
- **Role:** The strongman of the rival neighborhood.
- **Core traits:** Proud, loud, competitive, fiercely loyal to his own side.
- **Under pressure:** Escalates first and turns everything into a rivalry.
- **Weakness:** Pride and a short temper.
- **You're Abu Al-Nar if:** You're very competitive, you defend your team no matter what, and you can't let a challenge go.

### souad — سعاد / أم عصام (Souad, Um Issam)
- **Role:** Abu Issam's wife and Abu Shehab's sister.
- **Core traits:** Strong-willed, proud, outspoken, says what she thinks.
- **Under pressure:** Stands her ground, even when it costs her.
- **Weakness:** One sharp word too many.
- **You're Souad if:** You speak your mind, you don't let anyone talk down to you, and you have a strong sense of dignity.

### ferial — فريال (Ferial)
- **Role:** Mother of Lotfiya (Issam's wife).
- **Core traits:** Controlling, scheming, always has an opinion on other people's marriages.
- **Under pressure:** Stirs things up to get her way.
- **Weakness:** Creates more problems than she solves.
- **You're Ferial if:** You like to be in control and you always have something to say about everyone's life.
